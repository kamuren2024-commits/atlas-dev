# ENTERPRISE SERVICE CATALOG

This document lists all active production services, SLAs, and technical owners.

---

## 1. Active Services Inventory

Our platform runs 4 core enterprise services:

* **Sourcing Scribe Service**: Configures bid drafting and PPADA compliant requisition generation.
* **Compliance Auditor Service**: Scans active tenders against regulatory rule tables.
* **Digital Twin Simulator**: Collects substation sensor telemetry and executes Monte Carlo models.
* **On-Call Gateway**: Routes metrics and alerts directly to SRE On-Call staff.

---

* **Compliance Auditor**: Owned by `@SCM-Compliance` team.
