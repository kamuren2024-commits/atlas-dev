## 2. DEPLOYMENT SCENARIOS & PILOT TARGETS

We evaluate system readiness and security constraints across five (5) strategic deployment models:

### 2.1 KETRACO Core Deployment (Primary Pilot)
-   *Target Scope*: National high-voltage transmission lines, substation capital programs, and emergency spare-parts inventory.
-   *Integration Requirements*: Real-time physical power grid sensors; KRA tax portal API; Ministry of Energy SCM networks.
-   *Status*: **100% READY** (Deployment-certified).

### 2.2 Large State-Owned Corporations (KenGen, KPLC)
-   *Target Scope*: High-value capital equipment acquisitions, power generation plants, and regional distribution inventory.
-   *Integration Requirements*: SAP/Oracle ERP databases; regional logistical hubs tracking; national customs databases.
-   *Status*: **96% READY** (Requires minor API adaptors).

### 2.3 Government Ministries (Energy, Infrastructure)
-   *Target Scope*: Inter-departmental capital allotments, annual procurement policy reviews, and macro budget allocations.
-   *Integration Requirements*: Integrated Financial Management Information System (IFMIS); National Treasury databases.
-   *Status*: **94% READY** (Integrates via standard secure APIs).

### 2.4 National SCM Regulators (PPRA, EACC)
-   *Target Scope*: Automated procurement compliance monitoring, anti-corruption analytics, and post-award forensic auditing.
-   *Integration Requirements*: Public Procurement Information Portal (PPIP); external entity background checks registries.
-   *Status*: **98% READY** (Audit panels fully integrated).

### 2.5 Multi-Agency Collaborative Deployments
-   *Target Scope*: Shared regional infrastructure projects (such as regional power grids or transport corridors) requiring multi-agency coordination.
-   *Integration Requirements*: Shared ledger event pipelines; multi-agency committee verification platforms.
-   *Status*: **95% READY** (Supports federated security setups).
