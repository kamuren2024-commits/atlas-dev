## 2. STRATEGIC DECISION EVIDENCE CHAINS

To ensure regulatory compliance and financial transparency, the platform establishes clear evidence chains around eight (8) high-level operational actions:

### 2.1 Procurement Decisions
-   **Traceability Path**: Requisition URN $\rightarrow$ Tender Specifications Hash $\rightarrow$ Evaluation scoring matrix $\rightarrow$ Professional Opinion Form SCM-09 $\rightarrow$ Accounting Officer sign-off.
-   **Verification Standard**: Confirms that procurement is linked to an approved requisition and has been awarded to the lowest evaluated bidder.

### 2.2 Budget Utilization & Disbursements
-   **Traceability Path**: Treasury Allotment $\rightarrow$ Project commitment ledger $\rightarrow$ Milestone verification certificates $\rightarrow$ Payment voucher $\rightarrow$ Completed Bank transaction hash.
-   **Verification Standard**: Eliminates unauthorized expenditures, ensuring all capital expenditures are matched to approved project milestones.

### 2.3 Savings Claims & Cost Reductions
-   **Traceability Path**: Initial market index price $\rightarrow$ Negotiated bid totals $\rightarrow$ Final contract price $\rightarrow$ Completed material delivery receipts showing variance.
-   **Verification Standard**: Validates value-for-money claims, ensuring savings are supported by public price indices.

### 2.4 Supplier Selections & Capacity Audits
-   **Traceability Path**: Bid submission data $\rightarrow$ Financial capacity audits $\rightarrow$ Machinery check reports $\rightarrow$ Active SCM workload indicators.
-   **Verification Standard**: Proves that selected suppliers possess the technical and financial capacity required for project execution.

### 2.5 Contract Variations & Price Escalations
-   **Traceability Path**: Base legal instrument $\rightarrow$ Project engineer variation request $\rightarrow$ Cost benefit study $\rightarrow$ SCM Committee approval $\rightarrow$ Head of SCM and Legal sign-off.
-   **Verification Standard**: Confirms that variation contracts comply with PPADA Section 139 limitations and do not exceed the statutory 25% cost variance cap.

### 2.6 Inventory Movements & Allocations
-   **Traceability Path**: Supplier invoice validation $\rightarrow$ Warehouse Good Received Note (GRN) $\rightarrow$ Bin location logs $\rightarrow$ Materials dispatch requisition $\rightarrow$ Field installation receipt.
-   **Verification Standard**: Ensures all physical inventory movements are matched to real project requirements, preventing inventory slippage.

### 2.7 Project Delays & Critical Path Shifts
-   **Traceability Path**: Target milestone schedule $\rightarrow$ Delayed transit GPS logs $\rightarrow$ Site engineer delay warnings $\rightarrow$ Re-calculated Gantt critical-path dependencies.
-   **Verification Standard**: Proves that project schedule shifts are supported by physical operational data and logistics exceptions, eliminating artificial delay excuses.

### 2.8 Risk Escalations & Mitigations
-   **Traceability Path**: Risk identification ticket $\rightarrow$ Monte Carlo exposure score $\rightarrow$ Recommended mitigation sequence $\rightarrow$ Verification signatures.
-   **Verification Standard**: Quantifies the financial and scheduling impact of deployed risk mitigations, showing ROI on proactive capital investments.
