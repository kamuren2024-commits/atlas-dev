# EXECUTIVE CONTRACT CENTER DESIGN SPECIFICATION

## 1. Introduction
The **Executive Contract Center** is the primary interface through which KETRACO’s Managing Director, Head of Supply Chain, and Board Auditors monitor and govern the contract portfolio. The dashboard is designed to provide maximum clarity, high visual density, and rapid compliance tracking.

---

## 2. Interface Layout & Navigation
The dashboard is split into four primary functional zones:

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ZONE 1: Header - Status Indicators, Portfolio Totals, Active Holds    │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ZONE 2: Left Panel              │  ZONE 3: Right Panel                │
│  Interactive Contract Twins      │  Living Digital Twin Details,       │
│  List & Quick Search Filters     │  Causal Risk Simulator & Caches     │
├────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  ZONE 4: Bottom Panel - Multi-Agent Negotiation Sandbox, Compliance    │
│  Locks, and Interactive HITL Signature Handshake Form                  │
└────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### 2.1 Zone 1: Portfolio Executive Summary Banner
*   **Total Portfolio Commitment**: Overall financial scale of all tracked contracts.
*   **Total Contingency Allocation**: Remaining safety funds across active projects.
*   **Active PPADA Statutory Holds**: Clear count of contracts currently paused awaiting board review or compliance certificates.
*   **Global Portfolio Health Score**: Aggregated Contract Health Index.

### 2.2 Zone 2: Interactive Contract Registry
*   Lists all active contracts with search, category filtering, and color-coded risk indicators.
*   Shows quickly scannable stats for each contract: Title, Supplier, active CHI, and current variation level.

### 2.3 Zone 3: Live Digital Twin Details
*   **Dynamic Visual Timeline**: Interactive milestones, payments, and deliverables.
*   **Contract Knowledge Graph**: Interactive visual tree displaying relationships and multi-hop dependency risks.
*   **Causal Risk Predictor**: Evaluates down-stream delays if selected materials or logistics paths are disrupted.

### 2.4 Zone 4: Negotiation Sandbox & Compliance Gate
*   **What-If Scenario Dropdowns**: Allows operators to simulate price surges, geotechnical shifts, or time extensions.
*   **Sovereign Signature Lock**: Mandatory input area requiring co-signature approvals, justification remarks, and PIN validations (Code: 1234) before releasing contract holds.
