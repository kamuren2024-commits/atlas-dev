## Salience Atlas Autonomous Procurement Operating System (APOS)

This document maps all operational milestones of KETRACO procurement workflows directly to statutory Kenyan guidelines and constitutional principles.

---

### Constitutional SCM Principles (Article 227)

Article 227 of the Constitution of Kenya 2010 mandates that public procurement must be carried out in a system that is:
* **Fair:** Equitable treatment of all bidders; no arbitrary exclusion.
* **Equitable:** Fostering development through preferences and reservations.
* **Transparent:** Clear, disclosed criteria and accessible records.
* **Competitive:** Open tender as the default sourcing standard.
* **Cost-effective:** Maximum value for public funds.
