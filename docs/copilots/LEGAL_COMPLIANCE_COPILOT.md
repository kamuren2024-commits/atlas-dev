# LEGAL COMPLIANCE COPILOT SPECIFICATION

This document outlines the PPADA-2015 audit trees, legal citation indices, and risk scoring led by the Legal Compliance Copilot.

---

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The Legal Compliance Copilot enforces absolute compliance with procurement statutes, protecting KETRACO from litigation risks.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities

* **Dynamic PPADA Auditor**: Scans tender bid criteria against active Kenyan public procurement regulations.
* **Contract Term Reviewer**: Flags high-risk clauses or missing standard indemnification sections in contract drafts.
* **Compliance Citation Indexer**: Generates precise citations tracking bid scores directly to governing regulatory clauses.
