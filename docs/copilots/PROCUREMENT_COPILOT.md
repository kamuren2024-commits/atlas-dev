# PROCUREMENT COPILOT SPECIFICATION

This document details the configuration, prompt schemas, and capability registers of the KETRACO SCM Procurement Copilot.

---

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The Procurement Copilot assists sourcing professionals with requisition drafting, tender scoring, and supplier evaluation.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities

* **Interactive Requisition Scribe**: Translates raw text requirements into structured tender proposals conforming to PPADA templates.
* **Dynamic Cost Estimator**: Correlates historic line-item costs to flag inflated bid submissions.
* **Supplier Risk Analyst**: Surfaces supplier affiliation alerts and past delivery delays.
