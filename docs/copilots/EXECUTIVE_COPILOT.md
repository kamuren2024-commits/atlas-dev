# EXECUTIVE COPILOT SPECIFICATION

This document outlines the strategic analytics, financial forecasts, and high-level reports compiled by the Executive Copilot.

---

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The Executive Copilot aggregates data across SCM, finance, and operations to assist KETRACO directors with strategic planning.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities

* **Strategic KPI Aggregator**: Synthesizes power capacity margins, procurement SLA timelines, and budget balances.
* **FinOps Forecasting Simulator**: Models future cloud infrastructure spending and AI token cost projections.
* **Interactive Briefing Scribe**: Generates polished, executive-ready summary decks and reports.
