# SUPPLIER INTELLIGENCE COPILOT SPECIFICATION

This document details the vendor telemetry, rating systems, and bidding trend checkers of the Supplier Intelligence Copilot.

---

## 1. Overview & Objectives

The Supplier Intelligence Copilot aggregates corporate profiles, financial health markers, and delivery track records to audit supplier trust.

---

## 2. Key Capabilities

* **Lexical Identity Resolver**: Merges duplicate corporate entities using registration records and VAT PIN indexes.
* **SLA Performance Tracker**: Logs and visualizes supplier lead-time compliance and delivery quality scores.
* **Collusion Grouping Scanner**: Flags clusters of bidding entities sharing metadata or IP footprints.
