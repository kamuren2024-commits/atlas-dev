# SCM BUSINESS GLOSSARY

This is the official business glossary defining key terms used across KETRACO procurement and engineering teams.

---

## 1. Glossary Registry

### A. Substation Load Factor
* **Definition**: Ratio of average electrical load to peak system capacity over a specified time block.
* **Business Unit**: Grid Engineering
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Avg MW}}{\text{Peak MW}} \times 100$

### B. Procurement Bid Deviation
* **Definition**: Percentage difference between a supplier's bid price and KETRACO's internal engineering estimate.
* **Business Unit**: Legal Procurement
* **Formula**: $\frac{\text{Bid Price} - \text{Internal Estimate}}{\text{Internal Estimate}} \times 100$
