# Enterprise Governance Compliance Charter

This charter details KETRACO's SCM Intelligence Nexus corporate and legal compliance policies.

---

## 1. Regulatory Alignment

* **Public Procurement and Asset Disposal Act (PPADA, Kenya)**: All agentic audit checks are calibrated against the PPADA standards, evaluating bid transparency, technical capacity ratios, and legal registrations.
* **National Critical Infrastructure Protection Bill**: Ensures all substation coordinates, transmission path specs, and grid loading limits are treated as critical national security parameters.

---

## 2. Dynamic Verification & Compliance Audits

The platform enforces governance-as-code through:
* Mandatory pre-commit linter checks.
* Automated pre-boot scanning for credential exposures.
* Live hashing monitors detecting environmental manipulations.
