## 💡 Command Center Overview

The Enterprise Command Center centralizes system status, telemetry, and business intelligence into an integrated control dashboard:

* **Unified Status Metrics**: Aggregates grid operational health, bid compliance rates, and cloud budget allocations.
* **Geographic Map Layers**: Renders active substations and shipping lines with localized thermal overlays.
* **Automated Incident Triggers**: Defines explicit SEV 1 through SEV 4 incidents with pre-defined SLA response gates.
* **Executive Summary Slides**: Exposes real-time summaries optimized for presentation displays and scheduled PDF delivery.
