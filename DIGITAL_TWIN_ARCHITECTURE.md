## 💡 Digital Twin Architecture Summary

We model and simulate critical KETRACO assets to predict and mitigate supply chain constraints:

* **Asset Modeling**: Configures health indexes, operational temperatures, oil levels, and load factors for key transformers and lines.
* **Process Twins**: Represents RFP lifecycles, scoring paths, and compliance audits to map operational velocities and eliminate bottlenecks.
* **Topology Dependencies**: Computes cascading grid failures dynamically when central substation nodes (such as Suswa or Isinya) experience constraints.
* **What-If Analysis**: Executes Monte Carlo simulation models to forecast 90-day spare lead-times, re-routing deliveries around geopolitical bottlenecks.
