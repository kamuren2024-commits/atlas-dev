## 🏆 Summary of Accomplishments

The KETRACO SCM platform has been successfully transformed into a robust, secure, and compliant **Autonomous Enterprise Operating System**. SCM operations—from drafting requisitions and auditing bids to forecasting grid life-ratings and scheduling dispatches—can now be executed autonomously under safe, explainable, and multi-tiered human governance controls.
