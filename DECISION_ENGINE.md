## 💡 Decision Engine Architecture

The SCM Decision Intelligence Engine automates complex compliance audits and supply-chain reorders:

* **Risk Scoring Model**: Integrates bidder blacklists, price deviation metrics, and delivery SLA performance into composite risk scorecards.
* **Sourcing Optimization**: Ranks alternative suppliers using cost, timeline, and historic reliability vectors.
* **Explainable Output**: Generates human-readable evidence chains tracking every recommendation to specific regulatory clauses.
* **WORM Ledger Logging**: Writes decision outcomes directly to write-once-read-many (WORM) storage configurations to prevent tampering.
